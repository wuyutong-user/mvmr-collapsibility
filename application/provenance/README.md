# Historical application records

These scripts and tables document reporting work before the 6 October 2026
Collider revision. They are not current reporting entry points and may produce
earlier role summaries or adjustment labels. Their historical project inputs
are not all included here. To inspect an earlier project, configure
`IJE_HISTORICAL_PROJECT_ROOT` or use the working-directory convention of that script.

Use `../remap_collider_reporting.py` and `../export_reporting.py` for the current
168-row reporting register. The corrected adjustment counts are 5/129/22/12.
No Independent-cause decision is changed by the reporting revision.

Public-release path edits are recorded in `../../public_source_provenance.csv`.
