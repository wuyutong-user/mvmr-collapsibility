Supplementary Data 1 — public triad-level reporting fields

Source: Supplementary_Data_1_application_audit_168_v7_20260922.csv in the existing project archive.
Rows: 168 BMI–candidate-trait–cardiovascular outcome triads.

Fields:
triad_id: existing unique triad identifier.
candidate_trait: abbreviation used in Supplementary Tables S6–S11.
outcome: cardiovascular outcome abbreviation used in the Supplement.
working_structural_class: prespecified display/working label, not proof of an identified true DAG.
mediator_like_flag: TRUE/FALSE for the additional mediator-like pathway flag.
target_specific_adjustment_label: operational label under the prespecified application mapping for the BMI total-effect target.
observed_total_marginal_logOR_response: same-set unadjusted minus adjusted BMI logOR coefficient.
minimum_conditional_F: minimum conditional F statistic.

The structural and residual-scale response components were not separately identified.
Working adjustment labels remain conditional on retained-instrument validity. The labels do not establish that candidate omission preserves the exclusion restriction.
Revision dated 6 October 2026: the nine Collider adjustment labels were remapped from Overadjustment to Unclassified because the working Collider role alone does not establish the consequence of SNP-vector inclusion in summary-data MVMR. Counts: Necessary 5, Unnecessary 129, Overadjustment 22, Unclassified 12. All working roles, response values and conditional F statistics are unchanged. The Independent-cause mapping and its retained-instrument qualification are unchanged. The frozen methodological audit is preserved as historical context; this revision records the Collider resolution separately.
